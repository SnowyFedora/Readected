Readected v1.3.1 — build

Dependencies (Arch):
  sudo pacman -S qt6-base qt6-declarative qt6-quickcontrols2 \
                 poppler-qt6 cmake ninja pkgconf base-devel

Build & install:
  cd readected
  chmod +x install.sh INSTALL_HOME.sh
  ./INSTALL_HOME.sh    # installs to ~/.local (no root)
  # or: ./install.sh   # installs to /usr (needs sudo/pkexec)

Run:
  ~/.local/bin/readected
  readected /path/to/file.pdf
  readected-updater
